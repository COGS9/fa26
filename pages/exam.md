# Exams

There will be **two, non-cumulative exams** in this class (please mark them in your calendar!):
* **Monday, Oct 19** — Exam 1
* **Monday, Nov 23** — Exam 2

Each exam will be roughly **35 multiple-choice questions**, and will cover material from both the lecture and readings. The TAs will hold exam review sessions during discussion section before each exam.

## What happens if I miss an exam or have testing accommodations?
We will use the [Triton Testing Center (TTC)](https://tritontesting.ucsd.edu/) located in Pepper Canyon Hall 364 to administer exams for students with documented accommodations and for legitimate excused absences (does NOT include voluntary travel).

If you know you need to miss an exam for a legitimate, unavoidable conflict, please let **Dr. T** know ASAP. You may schedule your make-up exam **within a 1-week window** after the initial exam date. (Those with documented testing accommodations must schedule to take the exam the same day as everyone else.)
